# ✫ About Me

Hey, I'm **Steph**, better known as **systemCall()** — a Computer Systems Engineering graduate from the **University of Zambia**, currently working at **Airtel Zambia** while building software solutions that solve real-world problems.

I'm interested in the intersection of software, networking, telecommunications, and intelligent systems. I enjoy turning ideas into practical applications, learning how systems work under the hood, and building products that people can actually use.

I believe in learning by building. Most of my growth comes from experimenting, solving problems, and pushing my projects beyond the development stage into real deployments.
